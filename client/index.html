True state (dev)

Counter shift, 9 AM to 5 PM

You're the only one working the counter at a copy and print shop today.

  • Counter: customers line up to place orders or pick them up. Take the order (or turn it away if you can't make their time), and ring them up when they come back.
  • Orders: send each order to a printer that can handle it, then finish and bag it once it's printed. Some customers wait in the store, some come back later, some pick up tomorrow. Online orders come in on their own, already paid, and can't be turned away. Full service has a $2 service fee unless the printing is over $50; orders over $50 due the same day add a 10% rush fee.
  • Self-serve: most customers with simple jobs on regular paper make their own copies at the self-serve copiers, for less. Some come to the counter anyway and need to be shown over; some want full service.
  • Shipping: ship packages (box them if they need it), take prepaid drop-offs, check in the morning delivery and hand held packages to their owners. The carrier truck comes once, at 4:15 PM, and only waits 10 minutes: anything not handed off misses it, and express packages that miss it get refunded.
  • Phone: it rings for 30 seconds, then goes to voicemail. Answer it when you can; quote calls often turn into web orders. You can't pick up in the middle of helping someone at the counter.
  • Machines: printers run on their own, but they run out of paper and toner and they jam. Only you can fix that, with whatever is in the stockroom. There are no deliveries today, so don't take on work you can't print. A printer can also break down until a technician comes; recall its jobs and send them elsewhere. Keep the self-serve copiers fed and unjammed too, or the customers using them give up.

Everything takes time and you can only do one thing at once. Finishing work can be put down to help the counter; your progress is kept.

How to play: click anything in the store (a printer, the register, a customer at the counter, the phone) to walk there and see what you can do. Your current task is at the top; you can stop it.

Space pauses. Keys 1 to 4 set the speed. Escape closes a menu. Hover a greyed-out action to see why it's unavailable.
